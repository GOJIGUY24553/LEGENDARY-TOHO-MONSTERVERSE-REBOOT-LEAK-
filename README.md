# LEGENDARY-TOHO-MONSTERVERSE-REBOOT-LEAK-
Legendary Pictures Reportedly Exploring a Complete MonsterVerse Reboot in Partnership With Toho: THE 'GREATEST HITS' TRILOGY.

Following the critical and commercial success of Godzilla Minus One (2023) and Godzilla Minus Zero (2026), Legendary Pictures is reportedly in early discussions with Toho about taking the franchise in an entirely new direction. Sources allegedly familiar with the discussions claim that, following the release of Godzilla x Kong: Supernova (2027), Legendary may bring the MonsterVerse to a close and replace it with a new, standalone Godzilla trilogy.

The concept reportedly dates back to 2023, when Legendary first explored the possibility of developing a more grounded, character-driven interpretation of the franchise. However, plans were ultimately shelved as Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire moved further into production. According to the leak, the idea has since been revisited, with preliminary scripts for all three films allegedly already in development.

The proposed trilogy would reportedly take a different approach from the existing MonsterVerse, drawing more heavily from the themes, tone, and mythology of Toho's original films. Rather than continuing the shared cinematic universe built around Godzilla and Kong, the new continuity would place greater emphasis on Godzilla's origins as a consequence of nuclear warfare, his destructive relationship with humanity, and the larger implications of his existence. The intention is reportedly to create a more definitive interpretation of the character, combining recognizable elements from across the franchise's history into a cohesive, self-contained story.

Several factors are believed to have influenced the proposal. The success of Godzilla Minus One demonstrated that audiences and critics remain receptive to a more serious, emotionally grounded Godzilla film, while the MonsterVerse's increasingly fantastical direction has left room for a different interpretation of the property. Toho's continued interest in expanding Godzilla's international presence may also make a second major cinematic continuity appealing, particularly if the project is developed with substantial creative oversight from the Japanese studio.

The trilogy is allegedly being envisioned as a cinematic "Greatest Hits" of the franchise, incorporating some of its most recognizable monsters and story elements while reworking them into a single narrative. Legendary secured the rights to Godzilla and King Ghidorah in 2019, while a more recent licensing agreement is said to have granted the studio access to Destoroyah. The latter was initially considered for a third Godzilla x Kong installment, but was reportedly removed from the project as plans for the new trilogy took shape.

Although no official announcement has been made, the reported direction would represent a significant shift in Legendary's relationship with the franchise. Rather than simply continuing the MonsterVerse indefinitely, the studio would be looking to establish a new continuity with a defined beginning and end, one that draws from Godzilla's nearly seventy-year history while attempting to deliver what insiders allegedly describe as the "Definitive Godzilla." An idea to have this trilogy and the MonsterVerse is also reportedly being discussed as to increase the breadth and scope of the franchise.
